## Why this matters

A kitchen is where a hackerspace's conversations happen. Right now coffee means leaving the building, and meetup snacks arrive in plastic bags with nowhere to go. A working corner with a real espresso machine is the cheapest upgrade to how long people stay and how much they talk.

## What we will buy

- A bean-to-cup or semi-automatic espresso machine and a grinder
- A fridge with freezer compartment, a kettle, a microwave
- A water filter jug or under-sink filter
- Shelves, containers, a dish rack, mugs and glasses for 30 people
- A starter supply of beans, tea and snacks, and a jar for contributions
