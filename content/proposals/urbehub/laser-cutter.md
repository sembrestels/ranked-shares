## Why this matters

Half of what a hackerspace builds is flat: front panels, enclosures, stands, signs, badges, stencils. A laser cutter makes all of it in minutes from a vector file. Together with the 3D printer it closes the loop from idea to physical object without leaving the space, and it is the tool that most often gets a passer-by to sign up.

## What we will buy

- A desktop diode or small CO2 laser (400 x 400 mm bed class) with a safety enclosure
- Fume extractor with carbon filter and a window vent kit
- Honeycomb bed, air assist, fire blanket and CO2 extinguisher next to the machine
- A starter stock of 3 mm plywood, 3 mm acrylic and cardboard

## Rules of use

- Never left running unattended; a trained member is present for every job
- Materials list on the wall: what may be cut and what may not (no PVC, no unknown plastics)
- Two named operators train new users in a 30-minute session before their first job
