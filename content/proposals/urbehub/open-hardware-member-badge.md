## Why this matters

A badge is the classic hackerspace project: it teaches PCB design, soldering and firmware, gives every member something they built themselves, and becomes the object people hand to visitors. Adding a small secure-element demo makes it a teaching tool for how a hardware wallet signs.

## What we will do

- Design a badge around an ESP32 or RP2040 with an e-paper or OLED display, a few buttons and a battery, in an open source CAD tool
- Produce 60 kits: PCBs, components, and a laser-cut or printed case from the hub's own machines
- Firmware with a name display, a hub schedule screen and a signing demo
- Publish schematics, layout, firmware and the assembly guide under an open hardware licence

## Milestones

### A - Design and prototype - 500 EURC

- [ ] Schematic and layout published, 5 prototypes assembled and photographed
- [ ] Firmware with the three screens running on a prototype

### B - Production and workshop - 900 EURC

- [ ] 60 kits produced and cased
- [ ] Assembly workshop held with at least 20 badges soldered and working
- [ ] Repository with the full design and assembly guide linked from the hub wiki
