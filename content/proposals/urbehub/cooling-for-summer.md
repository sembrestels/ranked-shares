## Why this matters

The space is at its best in spring and autumn and nearly empty in summer, exactly when students and remote workers have the most free time. One split unit or two good portable units keep the room at a working temperature and keep the machines from overheating.

## What we will buy

- One split air conditioner (12,000 BTU class) installed by a licensed technician, subject to the landlord's approval, or
- Two portable units with window kits if a split cannot be installed
- A ceiling fan for the small room and blackout blinds for the sun-facing windows
