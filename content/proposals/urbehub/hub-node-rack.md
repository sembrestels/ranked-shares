## Why this matters

An Ethereum hackerspace should run Ethereum. A node in the room gives members a local RPC that never rate-limits, a validator that the whole community can watch and learn from, and a hands-on way to teach what a client actually does. A home server next to it hosts the things a space needs and nobody wants to pay a cloud bill for.

## What we will buy

- A fanless or low-noise mini PC (8-core, 64 GB RAM, 4 TB NVMe) for the execution and consensus clients
- A second small server (32 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD) for the wiki, a CI runner, a file share and a Home Assistant instance
- A small rack or shelf, a managed switch, a UPS, and cable management
- One year of a fixed IP or a tunnel for the public endpoint

The validator stake is not part of this proposal; the hub deposits it separately or joins a pooled staking setup.
