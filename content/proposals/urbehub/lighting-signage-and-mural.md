## Why this matters

The room looks like an office at night and is invisible from the street. Lighting changes how long people stay, and a sign and a mural tell the neighbourhood that this is an open community space, not a closed company.

## What we will do

- Replace the tube lighting with dimmable warm LED panels and add desk lamps
- Cut an outdoor sign on the hub laser cutter or order one, with the hub name and opening hours
- Commission a mural from a Rome-based artist through an open call among members
- Add LED strips and a projector-friendly wall for events
