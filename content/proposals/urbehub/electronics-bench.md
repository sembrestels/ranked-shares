## Why this matters

Members bring hardware wallets, dev boards and sensor projects to the hub and end up soldering on the kitchen table with a borrowed iron. There is no way to measure a signal, no stable power supply, and no stock of the resistors and headers every board needs. A bench costs less than a laptop and unlocks a whole class of projects.

## What we will buy

- Soldering station with temperature control and a hot-air rework station
- Bench power supply (30 V, 5 A, current limited)
- Entry-level 2-channel oscilloscope and a logic analyser
- Two multimeters, a magnifier lamp, helping hands, fume extractor
- Component drawers stocked with resistors, capacitors, headers, jumper wires, common ICs, USB cables, and an ESP32 and RP2040 board each
- ESD mat and wrist straps
