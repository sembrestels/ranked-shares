## Why this matters

People stay in a space where they can sit for eight hours. The current mix of donated chairs and folding tables is fine for an evening meetup and painful for a work day, which is exactly the use that makes a hackerspace a home base rather than an event venue.

## What we will buy

- 10 task chairs with adjustable height, lumbar support and armrests
- 2 electric height-adjustable desks
- 6 monitor arms and 4 used 27-inch monitors
- Power strips with USB-C PD at every desk, and cable trays
