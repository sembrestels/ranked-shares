## Why this matters

Hackathons are how the hub turns visitors into members and ideas into repositories. A small local edition, at the space, with mentors from the community, costs a fraction of a big event and produces projects that keep being worked on in the room where they started.

## What we will do

- Two days, Saturday morning to Sunday evening, at the hub
- Breakfast, lunch and dinner for 40 people on both days
- Four mentors with a stipend, covering Solidity, frontend, ZK and hardware
- Prizes: three small pools, decided by a member vote at the demo session
- Every project in a public repository under an open source licence

## Milestones

### A - Preparation - 600 EURC

- [ ] Date, theme, mentors and rules published at least 4 weeks before
- [ ] Registration open, with at least 30 sign-ups before the event

### B - The event - 3,000 EURC

- [ ] Event held with at least 30 participants and at least 8 projects demoed
- [ ] Prizes paid, all repositories linked from a public page, expense sheet posted
