## Why this matters

Members ask for the same things every month: how to write a contract, how a ZK circuit works, how to solder, how to keep keys safe. Regular workshops answer that at scale, give members a reason to come on a fixed evening, and give the hub a library of recordings.

## What we will do

- Six evenings, one per month, 2 hours each, at the hub
- Topics: Solidity from zero, a first ZK circuit, solder a board, model and print a part, personal opsec and key management, run an Ethereum node
- Each facilitator receives a stipend and a materials budget for kits
- Each session recorded and published with the slides and exercises
