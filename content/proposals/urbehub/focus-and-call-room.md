## Why this matters

Every day someone takes a call in the main room and everyone else goes quiet, or the caller steps into the corridor. A treated room with decent audio lets the hub host remote meetings, record talks and interviews, and gives members a quiet place when the main room is loud.

## What we will buy

- Acoustic panels for the walls and ceiling, a rug, and a door sweep and seal
- A USB microphone, a 1080p webcam, a key light and a small monitor
- A desk, two chairs and a whiteboard
- A door sign and a shared booking calendar

## Milestones

### A - Room treated - 1,200 EURC

- [ ] Panels, rug and door seal installed, before and after noise measurement published
- [ ] Desk, chairs and whiteboard in place

### B - Call setup - 600 EURC

- [ ] Microphone, camera, light and monitor installed and a test recording published
- [ ] Booking calendar live and linked from the wiki, with the house rules on the door
