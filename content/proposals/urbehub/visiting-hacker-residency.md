## Why this matters

The fastest way to raise the level of a space is to bring in people who have built things elsewhere. A week is long enough to work together, not just to give a talk. In return the hub gets a talk, a written guide or a shared project, and a link to another community.

## What we will do

- Open call for residents, chosen by member vote; priority to people from other hackerspaces and Ethereum builder communities
- Each resident receives travel and 7 nights of accommodation in Rome, and a desk at the hub
- Each resident gives one public talk or workshop and writes a short post about the week
- Residents are spread over 6 months so the space always has one recent visit to talk about
