## Why this matters

A hackerspace collects knowledge as well as tools. A small library gives students something to pick up while they wait for a build, gives members a quiet corner, and gives the space a memory. The books are chosen by the members and the shelf grows with donations.

## What we will buy

- Two bookshelves, two armchairs, a small table and two reading lamps
- About 40 books chosen by a member poll: cryptography, distributed systems, Ethereum, electronics, hardware design, and a few classics of hacker culture
- A sign-out sheet and bookplates
