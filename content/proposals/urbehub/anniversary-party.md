## Why this matters

A community that never celebrates burns out. A party is where members bring friends, where people who left come back, and where the hub gets the photos and the stories it needs to recruit next year. The demo table and talks keep it about what people built.

## What we will do

- Rent tables, sound and lighting for one evening at the hub or a nearby venue
- Food and drinks for 100 people, with a vegetarian and vegan option
- A DJ or a member playlist and a small stage for five 5-minute talks
- A demo table of projects built at the hub during the year
- A photographer and a 2-minute recap video

## Milestones

### A - Preparation - 800 EURC

- [ ] Date, venue, talk line-up and demo list published at least 3 weeks before
- [ ] Deposits paid for sound, food and venue

### B - The party - 2,000 EURC

- [ ] Event held with at least 80 attendees, talks and demos as planned
- [ ] Photos and the recap video published within 2 weeks, and the expense sheet posted
