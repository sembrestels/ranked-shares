## Why this matters

A mainnet launch is a one-time chance to get developers to try a product. Arkiv's launch lands around Devcon, when the community is already travelling and looking for things to build. Community-run events are cheaper than the foundation running them, reach local developer groups the foundation does not, and give the launch a presence in three cities instead of one conference hall.

The events are hands-on by design: the measure of success is how many people leave with something written to Arkiv, not how many chairs were filled.

## What we will do

- **Three city meetups** hosted by local communities: Urbe Hub in Rome, and partner communities in Warsaw and Lisbon. Each evening: a 20-minute talk on Arkiv, a 90-minute workshop where attendees write and query their first records, and lightning demos of what people built.
- **Devcon side event**: a half-day workshop track with three sessions (first records, agent memory with the MCP server, building a small app), open to Devcon attendees, with the reference apps as the material.
- Every talk recorded and published; every workshop published as a self-guided tutorial afterwards.

## Budget

- Venues, food and drinks for about 50 people per city meetup and 100 at the side event
- Travel for one facilitator between the three cities
- Workshop material and a small prize per event for the best demo
- Recording and editing

## Requirements

- Each event publishes attendee count and the number of wallets that wrote to Arkiv during the workshop
- Talks and workshops published within two weeks, under an open licence
- Expense sheet published after the last event

## Milestones

### A - City meetups - 9,000 USDC

- [ ] Three meetups held with at least 30 attendees each and at least 15 wallets writing to Arkiv in each workshop
- [ ] Recordings and self-guided tutorials published

### B - Devcon side event - 11,000 USDC

- [ ] Side event held with at least 60 attendees and three workshop sessions delivered
- [ ] Recordings published, expense sheet for all four events posted
